Ending balances and risk

Each rule starts each window with 100 USDT and no BTC. Net return includes modeled execution costs. Drawdown measures the largest decline from a previous equity peak.

How the simulation works

Signals and fills

Rules see completed hourly candles and can trade at the next hourly open. The model assumes full fills and a single long position. It records remaining BTC at the modeled bid at the end of each window.

Trading rules can allocate up to 10% of equity per entry. The passive BTC benchmark can use the full available balance. Cash earns no yield.

Costs and coverage

The tables show the baseline and a fresh simulation at double the assumed fee, spread and slippage. Spread is the full bid to ask distance, so the model applies half that spread to either side.

Hourly bars cannot establish actual fill probability, queue position, partial fills or intrahour drawdowns. The model omits taxes, outages and CoinSwitch account fees. These trailing periods overlap and do not provide independent estimates of future performance.
